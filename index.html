About Us

GovConnect started from a simple frustration: finishing one government task often means opening five different websites and guessing which one is real.

We gather the steps, the documents you need and the official link in a single page, written for students, job seekers and anyone doing this for the first time.

Privacy and safety

GovConnect has no accounts, no tracking and no database. The contact form sends nothing, and reviews stay in your own browser.

Never share an OTP, Aadhaar or PAN number with this site or anyone who calls you. Only enter them on the official portal each guide links to.
